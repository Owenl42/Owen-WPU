About Me

I am an aspiring high school history teacher with a passion for education, history, and helping students understand the world around them. I hope to create an engaging, supportive classroom where students feel comfortable asking questions, examining different perspectives, and connecting historical events to the present.

My background in retail, hospitality, and team leadership has helped me develop communication, organization, patience, adaptability, and problem-solving skills. Working with people from diverse backgrounds has shown me the value of listening, collaboration, and meeting individual needs—qualities I hope to bring into my future classroom.

As I continue preparing for a career in education, my goal is to make history more than a collection of names and dates. I want students to see it as a tool for understanding society, thinking critically, and becoming informed members of their communities.
